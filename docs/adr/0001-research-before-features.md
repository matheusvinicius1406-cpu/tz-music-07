# ADR 0001: Research Before Feature Implementation

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The product depends on many public protocols, open-source references, and legally sensitive music-provider boundaries.

## Decision

No provider, resolver, importer, or player feature is implemented until the relevant `docs/research/` note exists with URL, license, evidence, extracted pattern, decision, and risks.

## Consequences

Early work is slower, but architecture decisions remain traceable and the project avoids accidental license or legal boundary violations.
